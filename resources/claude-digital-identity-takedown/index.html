Claude: Removing Your Digital Identity

Audit your exposed personal data, draft legally binding DMCA & privacy removal requests, and purge data brokers in an afternoon with Claude.

Search your full name on Google right now. Within seconds, data aggregator sites reveal your personal phone number, home address, family connections, and work history. You never consented to this indexing—data brokers scrape, bundle, and monetize your digital identity for public lookup.

Reclaiming your digital footprint used to require hundreds of dollars in automated removal services or dozens of frustrating manual form submissions. Using Claude as your automated privacy assistant, you can conduct a full exposure audit, draft binding removal notices, and clear your online identity in a single afternoon. Here is the step-by-step blueprint.

Step 1: Audit Your Exposed Digital Footprint

Run an incognito audit on your full name, location, past employers, and contact details to map every data broker currently selling your information.

I want to conduct a comprehensive audit of my publicly exposed personal information. Provide a systematic reconnaissance checklist covering specific Google search operators (exact match quotes, location filters, phone/email queries) and list the top 15 data broker aggregators I should check for active listings.

Step 2: De-Index Personal Data from Google Search

Utilize Google's "Results about you" tool at myactivity.google.com and submit targeted removal requests for indexed pages revealing personally identifiable information (PII).

Draft a formal, concise removal request to Google requesting the de-indexing of a webpage exposing my private contact details under their PII and privacy policies. Include placeholders for target URLs, specific exposed information (phone/address), and my contact verification.

Step 3: Issue Direct Takedowns to Data Brokers

Systematically execute removal requests targeting primary aggregators: Spokeo, Whitepages, BeenVerified, Radaris, Intelius, PeopleConnect, MyLife, PeopleFinders, and Acxiom.

Provide a prioritized opt-out guide for the major data brokers (Spokeo, Whitepages, BeenVerified, Radaris, Intelius, PeopleConnect, MyLife, PeopleFinders, Acxiom). For each broker, detail the direct opt-out URL or email contact method in a step-by-step checklist.
Write a firm, legally backed data deletion request email to send to data broker support teams. Cite applicable data privacy regulations and explicitly demand the full removal of my personal profile, contact records, and historical addresses. Keep it clear, professional, and actionable.

Step 4: Execute Copyright & Privacy DMCA Takedowns

If unauthorized photos or personal media appear on external websites, deploy legally structured DMCA takedown notices or personal privacy removal requests.

I own the copyright to a photograph published on an external website without my authorization. Draft a formal DMCA takedown notice to be sent to the website owner and hosting provider. Include standard good-faith statements, legal accuracy affirmations, and placeholders for image URLs and contact information.
