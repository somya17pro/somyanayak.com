5 AI Prompts for Self-Reflection

PROMPT 1

Ask me one question at a time until you believe you've identified the belief that's limiting me the most. Challenge your own conclusion before presenting it, then explain your reasoning.

PROMPT 2

Ignore my explanations. Analyze only my repeated choices, priorities, and contradictions. Tell me what patterns emerge, how confident you are, and what evidence could disprove your interpretation.

PROMPT 3

Based on everything I say, describe me as a stranger would after observing my actions for one year. Separate observable behavior from inference.

PROMPT 4

Imagine my life five years from now if I never change my current habits. Describe the likely outcome, identify the assumptions behind your prediction, and then tell me the smallest action that could change that trajectory.

PROMPT 5

Ask me the one question I'm most likely to avoid answering. After I respond, explain why that question matters, what my answer suggests, and what alternative interpretations are possible.
